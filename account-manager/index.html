Core Capabilities

Designed from the ground up for speed, reliability, and full control over all your Roblox accounts.

Multi-Instance Support

Instantly launch and manage multiple accounts simultaneously without client conflicts. View all your saved accounts in a clean, filterable grid or list layout.

Integrated Anti-AFK

Never get disconnected for idling again. Simply toggle the global Anti-AFK feature to keep all active instances running continuously in the background.

Session Validation

The manager automatically detects and flags accounts with expired security cookies (EXPIRED tag) so you always know which accounts need re-authentication.

Advanced Power Tools

Comes packed with extra utilities including Group managers, Chart statistics, Mixers, and Generators directly accessible from the sleek sidebar.

Instant Process Kill

Need to shut down fast? The "Kill Roblox" shortcut instantly terminates all active game clients safely and cleanly in less than a second.

Smart Search & Filtering

Never lose track of your main or alt accounts. Easily search your entire database by ID or Username, and toggle between grid and compact list views.

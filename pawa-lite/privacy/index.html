Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains what personal data is processed when you visit the Pawa-Lite website or use the Pawa-Lite desktop apps (Executor and External), and what your rights are. We try to collect as little as possible.

1. Who is responsible

The operator of Pawa-Lite (“Padawan985”, “we”) is responsible for the processing described here. The easiest way to reach us for any privacy request is our Discord server.

2. Website

Hosting and server logs

The website is hosted by Vercel Inc. When you open a page, the hosting provider processes technical data such as your IP address, the requested URL, date and time, and browser and device information. This is needed to deliver the site and keep it secure (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views and see which pages are used. Before an event is sent, our code removes the query string and the hash from the page address. Vercel describes this analytics service as cookieless and does not use it to track you across other sites. The website itself does not set cookies and does not use advertising trackers. Third-party providers named below may process data as described in their own policies.

Fonts

The site loads its fonts (Space Grotesk, Source Serif 4 and JetBrains Mono) from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser connects to Google’s servers, so Google receives your IP address and technical browser data. This is based on our legitimate interest in a consistent design (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

No accounts, no forms

You do not need an account to browse the site, and there is no contact form that stores your messages on our server.

3. Downloads and Discord

To download Pawa-Lite you are asked to join our Discord server first. Discord processes your data under its own privacy policy, and we only see what Discord shows server owners, such as your username and join time. The installer files are served from GitHub, so GitHub receives your IP address when you download. We have no control over how Discord and GitHub handle that data; please check their policies.

4. Desktop apps (Executor and External)

While a Pawa-Lite app is running, it regularly sends a short status message (“heartbeat”) to our server. It contains:

  • a hardware identifier (HWID) of your device,
  • the username used in the app, and
  • a timestamp of the last message.

We use this to show how many people are currently online and to keep the service running (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The data is stored in a Google Firebase Realtime Database in the European region (europe-west1, Belgium). A user counts as online if the last message is less than two minutes old.

Please note: the usernames of users who are currently online can be shown publicly on our status page. Use a name that does not identify you. Stored entries are not deleted automatically, but you can ask us to delete yours at any time (see section 7). Your HWID is only used to tell devices apart in this list and is not published.

The apps do not read or upload your files, passwords or Roblox account details through this feature.

5. Third parties

  • Vercel Inc. – hosting and web analytics
  • Google (Fonts) – delivery of website fonts
  • Google (Firebase) – storage of heartbeat data
  • GitHub – delivery of installer files
  • Discord – community server and download verification

Some of these providers are based in the USA. Where data is transferred there, this is done on the basis of the providers’ data transfer mechanisms such as standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

6. How long we keep data

Server logs and analytics data are kept by the providers for as long as they need them for the purposes above. Heartbeat entries stay in the database until we delete them or you ask us to.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. To use these rights, message us on Discord and tell us the username used in the app; for deletion of heartbeat data we may ask you for your HWID so we can find the right entry. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

Pawa-Lite is not meant for children under 13. If you think a child has sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the site or apps change. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
